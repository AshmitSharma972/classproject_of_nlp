Patient is a 29-year-old female presenting with urinary tract infection symptoms including burning micturition and increased frequency.
She is allergic to penicillin — prior exposure caused a rash and swelling of her face and lips consistent with an allergic reaction.
She also reports a mild intolerance to sulfa drugs, which previously caused nausea and stomach upset.
She has no other known drug allergies.
Given her penicillin allergy, she was prescribed nitrofurantoin 100 mg twice daily for 5 days.
She was advised to return if symptoms worsen or do not improve within 48 hours.
She was also told to maintain good hydration and to report any new rashes or breathing difficulty.
