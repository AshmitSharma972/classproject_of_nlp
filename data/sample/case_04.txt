A 27-year-old male presents with high-grade fever, chills, and bodyache for 4 days after returning from a rural trip.
The treating physician suspects possibly malaria given the travel history and cyclical nature of the fever.
The physician also wants to rule out typhoid, as the patient reports some abdominal discomfort and relative bradycardia.
A peripheral blood smear and Widal test have been ordered.
The diagnosis is uncertain at this point pending lab results.
The patient is being empirically managed with supportive care and antipyretics in the meantime.
