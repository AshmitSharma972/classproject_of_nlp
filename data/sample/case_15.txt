A 39-year-old male presents with recurrent episodes of high fever.
He reports that the fever started mildly three days ago, but it worsened significantly by the second day, reaching 104°F.
He recalls a similar problem last year around the same time, which was diagnosed as dengue fever at that time.
He is worried it may be dengue again given the similar presentation.
The fever has been associated with severe myalgia and retro-orbital headache, consistent with his prior episode.
He reports no rash yet, but the rash appeared on day 4 during his previous illness.
He has no chronic illnesses and takes no regular medications.
