A 31-year-old female presents with fever and throat pain for 2 days.
She reports taking Paracetamol 500 mg three times a day for 3 days on her own before visiting the clinic.
She also took ibuprofen 400 mg once yesterday for the pain.
Despite the medication, her fever persists and the throat pain has not improved significantly.
On examination, the throat is erythematous with tonsillar enlargement bilaterally.
She was prescribed amoxicillin 500 mg twice daily for 7 days and advised to continue Paracetamol as needed.
She was also told to increase fluid intake and rest adequately.
