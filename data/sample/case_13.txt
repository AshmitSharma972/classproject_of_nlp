A 63-year-old male with hypertension and type 2 diabetes presents with worsening leg swelling and fatigue for the past 2 weeks.
His father died of a heart attack at 58 and his mother had chronic kidney disease.
He has a personal history of a myocardial infarction 6 years ago, for which he underwent coronary stenting.
He is currently on metformin 1000 mg twice daily, losartan 50 mg once daily, and atorvastatin 40 mg at night.
He denies chest pain, orthopnea, or paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea.
Examination reveals bilateral pitting pedal edema up to the knees and elevated jugular venous pressure.
He denies missing any doses of his medications but admits poor dietary compliance over the past month.
