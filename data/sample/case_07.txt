A 52-year-old male with fatigue and breathlessness on exertion underwent a workup last week.
His CBC showed hemoglobin of 8.5 g/dL, consistent with moderate anemia; MCV was 72 fL suggesting microcytic anemia.
His fasting blood sugar was 180 mg/dL, raising concern for uncontrolled diabetes.
Serum ferritin was low at 8 ng/mL, confirming iron deficiency.
His HbA1c came back at 8.9%, indicating poor long-term glycemic control.
Liver function tests and kidney function tests were within normal limits.
He was started on iron supplements and referred to an endocrinologist for diabetes management.
