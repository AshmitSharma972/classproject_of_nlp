A 22-year-old female presents with a complex evolving illness.
Five days ago she started with fever and generalized bodyache.
Three days later she developed a dry cough and mild sore throat.
Yesterday she had chills and rigors lasting about 2 hours in the evening.
Today she reports worsening fatigue and decreased appetite.
She has not taken any medications throughout this illness.
Her travel history includes a visit to a malaria-endemic rural area 10 days ago.
Labs and a blood smear have been ordered to evaluate the temporal progression of her symptoms.
